System Implementation & Technical Architecture

          

            Comprehensive technical breakdown of the reverse-engineered eCourts request flow and the deterministic drafting engine.
          

        


        

          
          
          

            

              
              eCourts Request Flow Reproduction
            

            
    
              
  • Session & Cookie Handshake: Initializes SERVICES_SESSID and JSESSION cookies from /ecourtindia_v6/.
    • 
              
  • Dynamic Delimiter Extraction: The eCourts portal dynamically injects a security delimiter in versioned JavaScript bundles (components.js?v=...). Our client parses this bundle on initialization to extract both the delimiter string and rotating custom header key (e.g. Biure465: delimeter).
    • 
              
  • Stateful Token Synchronization: Extracts the initial 64-character SHA256 app_token and continuously synchronizes refreshed tokens returned in AJAX delimiters (#####token#####).
    • 
              
  • Offline CAPTCHA Recognition: Employs a self-contained ONNX-based OCR model (ddddocr) running locally on CPU in ~15ms with contrast enhancement preprocessing, completely bypassing external paid APIs.
    • 
              
  • Comprehensive Parsing: Beautifulsoup parses key-value tables, party advocates, hearing history, and decodes displayPdf(...) arguments into structured Pydantic models.
    • 
            

          


          
          

            

              
              Rule-Based Legal Drafting Engine
            

            
    
              
  • Strict Non-LLM Compliance: Implemented 100% in pure Python using deterministic legal template models and rule-based document assemblers.
    • 
              
  • Indian Legal Conventions: Generates court headers, memo of parties, chronological averments, statutory disclosures (e.g. Section 12A Commercial Courts Act pre-institution mediation), itemized prayers, verifications, and Statement of Truth under Order VI Rule 15A CPC.
    • 
              
  • Indian Numbering & Currency Engine: Custom converter for formal pleadings formatting (e.g. Rs. 24,50,000/- (Rupees Twenty-Four Lakh Fifty Thousand Only)).
    • 
              
  • Multi-Format Exports: Generates Microsoft Word (.docx with 1.5-inch court binding margins and 1.5 line spacing), printable legal HTML, clean Markdown, and structured JSON.
    • 
              
  • Cross-Task Synergy Bridge: A one-click prefill bridge that maps scraped eCourts cases directly into legal drafts.
